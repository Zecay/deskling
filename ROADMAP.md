# Roadmap

## Done

- GPU engine: 200 buddies at 60 fps in one small `.exe`
- Radial and dropdown menus, pins, group commands, tray icon and hotkeys
- Deskling Studio: looks, personality, physics, menu themes and Unleashed mode
- Import your own hats and glasses

## Next: 1.0

- Built-in pixel editor with per-pose animation preview
- Species packs: cats, ghosts, robots, slimes, your own
- Names, friendships and rivals, per-buddy settings
- Saved crews and profiles, like "Work" and "Party"
- Outlines, capes and held items
- Walk zones and allowed monitors
- Weather, seasonal themes and sound packs
- Code-signed releases, an installer and `winget`

## After 1.0

- **Good desktop citizen:** auto-hide in video calls and screen shares, Do Not Disturb schedule, reduced motion, app blocklist
- **Trigger rules without code:** "When Claude finishes, celebrate", "At 5 pm, everyone dances"
- Deeper Claude Code reactions to builds, tests and finished tasks
- Shareable data packs with validation and drag-and-drop import
- Mini-games: races, crew battles and collectibles
